One Cell, Every Traversal

 

Every traversal, in the pattern’s own frame

Each instance translated to put the turn at the origin and rotated to put the approach along the same bearing, measured in circle radii. This is what rotation-invariant features buy: the same pattern in forty places on the map becomes forty attempts at one thing.

Where they actually are

The same instances on the playfield, each cropped to its own three objects. Position on the map is the thing the overlay throws away.

Cell membership is a property of the beatmap, not of a play: angle, spacing and snap come from the map’s own geometry, so these are the same objects for every player and nobody chose which traversals to show. Membership is read from the binning package that produced the findings table, so the picture and the number cannot disagree about what is in the bin. Chirality is preserved rather than folded — this cell is one handedness band, and mirroring the other one in would erase the asymmetry the signed angle exists to keep.
