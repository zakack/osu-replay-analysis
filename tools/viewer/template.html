Slider bodies are lazer’s own piecewise-linear path, shipped by the extraction shim rather than refitted here — the curve is never recomputed in the browser. The ball walks that polyline by arc length, the one piece of arithmetic left to the viewer, checked against lazer’s nested object positions on 36,676 of them before any of this was written. Cursor samples are the recorded 60 Hz frames, interpolated between; a button change forces a frame, so every press time and press position is exact.

The hit error bar puts the beat down the middle, in map time across the meh window: each press drops a tick above the line where it landed against the beat, and the cursor drops one below where it was deepest in the circle. When the two hands move together an object’s two ticks stand one over the other. Every number in the inspector comes from the extraction shim’s own geometry and clicks passes over this replay; none is computed here.
