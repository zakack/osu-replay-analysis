Cell atlas

Each instance is one turn, drawn in the cell’s own frame: the apex at the origin, the approach rotated onto the axis pointing right, distances in circle radii. The features are rotation and scale invariant by construction, so turns from anywhere on the playfield superimpose exactly and a cell drawn from a CS4 map and a CS7 map is the same picture. Cell membership and the boundary both come from the binning package that produced the findings table, so the drawing and the number cannot disagree about what is in the bin or where its edges are. Chirality is preserved rather than folded: a turn below the axis went the other way round, and mirroring it in would erase the asymmetry the signed angle exists to keep.
